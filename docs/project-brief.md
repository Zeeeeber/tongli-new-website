3. Product Structure
==================================================

Main product categories:

1. Wood Veneer Panels
This is the most important category.
Wood veneer panels are decorative panels made by pressing natural wood veneer or engineered veneer onto substrates such as plywood, MDF, particle board, blockboard or OSB.
Examples:
- veneer plywood
- veneer MDF
- veneer particle board
- veneer blockboard
- natural veneer panel
- engineered veneer panel
- smoked veneer panel
- dyed veneer panel

2. Natural Wood Veneer
Natural veneer sheets in different wood species.
Examples:
- Basswood
- Sapele
- Teak
- Tunis
- Elm
- Sandalwood
- White Oak
- Red Oak
- Black Walnut
- Brazilian Rosewood
- Smoked Oak
- Smoked Eucalyptus
- Dyed natural veneer

3. Engineered Wood Veneer
Engineered / reconstituted veneer.
Main points:
- 300+ styles
- stable color
- consistent grain
- suitable for large-scale furniture, doors and interior projects

4. 3D Wood Panels
Decorative carved solid wood panels for wall decoration, doors, ceilings, furniture surfaces and commercial interiors.

Secondary product categories:

5. Veneer Edge Banding
Wood veneer strips cut into different widths for panel edge banding.

6. Melamine Board
Decorative boards similar to veneer panels, but the surface material is melamine paper instead of natural or engineered veneer.

7. Supporting Boards
Substrate and supporting board materials:
- Commercial Plywood
- Basswood Plywood
- Birch Plywood / Marine Plywood
- Fire-Retardant Plywood
- Raw MDF
- MR MDF / HDF
- Fire-Retardant MDF / HDF
- Particle Board
- OSB
- Blockboard

Important:
Do not make all products look equally important.
Wood Veneer Panels, Natural Wood Veneer, Engineered Wood Veneer and 3D Wood Panels are the main products.
Veneer Edge Banding, Melamine Board and Supporting Boards are secondary products.
